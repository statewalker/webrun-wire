"@statewalker/webrun-http-streams": patch

An idle body is cancelled as soon as its exchange is torn down. A served handler's response body with no chunk pending (an SSE stream between events, a long poll) was cancelled only once its next chunk came: the transport's `return()`/`throw()` on the served output is queued behind the `next()` waiting on `reader.read()`. Bodies are now read through a hand-written iterator whose `return()`/`throw()` cancel the stream directly, and the abandoned exchange cancels the served body with the abort's reason (`throw(error)`'s error, or the "abandoned" error for `return()`); the `next()` in flight settles as done. The cancel is started, never awaited, so a tee branch whose other branch nobody reads (`response.clone()`) no longer hangs the teardown either, nor the release of a HEAD/204 response's body. Likewise a served handler's pending read of its request body fails at once with that reason, a cancel of that body no longer waits for the next chunk, a caller's streamed upload is cancelled at once when its request's signal aborts, and `newHttpServerStub`/`newHttpClientStub` cancel an idle response body or upload at once on `return()`. Normal completion cancels nothing; the wire format is unchanged.
